# Volunteer page

## Scope and mode
`/volunteer` (and `/es/volunteer`). Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Inherits the established world (DESIGN.md). The Partner page (`/partner`) follows this layout for churches, businesses and organizations.

## Audience, job, action
- Medical professionals first (surgeons, anesthesia providers, nurses, surgical technologists, follow-up clinicians, counselors), then non-medical volunteers (interpreters, drivers, meals, office help, prayer team).
- Job: see why the ministry exists (the card's Vision), where their skills fit, what's asked of them, and how joining works; then offer their skills.
- Primary action: Volunteer your skills (the interest form on this page). Secondary: See the roles.

## Proof and content
The card's Vision is verbatim. User steer at the surface round: "I choose Vision First, but I love the part of A colleague's letter that has the testimonial of a volunteer surgeon. That should definitely be part of this page." The letter is a labeled sample ("Sample letter, not a real volunteer") until a real volunteer writes one. Roles, credential requirements, time, place, faith expectations and the joining steps are best guesses, marked Placeholder.

## Constraints
WCAG 2.2 AA, EN/ES structure, no invented claims, Dr. Franks only as contact information, demo forms send nothing.
