# Home page

## Scope and mode
The site's front door (`/`). Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The site-wide visual world starts here; every other page inherits it.

## Audience, job, action
- Ranked: people seeking help (adults, and parents or guardians of children); people referring someone; medical professionals; partners; donors.
- Job: within seconds, understand that this is a Christian surgical ministry offering reconstructive plastic surgery, free or at reduced cost, to children and adults in the Charlotte and Fort Mill area, open to everyone; then ask for help or refer someone.
- Primary action: Ask for help. Secondary: Refer someone. Give stays in the nav.

## Proof and content
Mission card text verbatim (PRODUCT.md). User direction, comp round: the mission statement sits directly above the six values so the values have context. Follow the card's order: mission, then the belief statement, "Therefore", and the six values. No stats, testimonials or photos exist; sample stories must carry the label "Sample — not a real patient".

## Constraints
HIPAA (no trackers on help pages), WCAG 2.2 AA, EN/ES structure, no link to WDF, placeholder wordmark, placeholder contact details.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Carolina potters glaze stoneware with wood ash, and the kiln turns that ash to glass. This is a true "beauty from ashes": fired, handmade, never repeated. It refuses the sunset-photo, icon-row ministry page.
OWN-WORLD: Buff salt-glazed stoneware ground with an orange-peel texture; iron-brown serif display; spaced-capital eyebrows; humanist sans for body; cobalt slip as the only action color, with hand-brushed edges on buttons and rules; amber and olive ash glaze appear only in photography.
STORY: Someone who needs surgery they can't afford learns what this is, that it's free or reduced-cost, local and open to everyone. They read the mission that gives the values their meaning, then ask for help or refer someone.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav: wordmark left, five links, EN | ES, cobalt Give. Left ~45%: eyebrow, three-line serif headline, paragraph, cobalt Ask for help, outlined Refer someone. Right ~55%: one monumental ash-glazed jar. Bottom band: OUR MISSION, a brushed cobalt dash, the three-line italic mission quotation.
FORM: Ash & Cobalt, rank 1 of 7 on the grounded list (Impeccable's pick), seed key 1ce77379. Signature interaction: cobalt brushstrokes draw themselves on as each section enters, like a slip-trailed line; static when reduced motion is on.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
