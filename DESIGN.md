# Design System: Beauty From Ashes Surgical Ministry

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Salt-Glazed Page"**

The page is a piece of Carolina stoneware. Its ground is buff salt-glazed clay with an orange-peel surface, tiled from a clean patch of the approved comp. Its ink is iron brown, the color of the clay body under the glaze: a warm book serif for everything the ministry says, a soft humanist sans for everything it explains. One material sits on top, cobalt slip brushed by hand. Cobalt marks what a visitor can do (ask, refer, give, follow a link) and draws the few ornamental lines: the rule under the header, the dash under a section label, the strokes beside the values, the rules between the steps, the dab beside the section being read, and the brushed stroke under every link outside the footer. Nothing else on the page is blue, and nothing else is bright.

The ash glaze that gives the ministry its name, amber and olive green running down a jar's shoulder, lives only inside photographs of fired pots: the hero jar, the glaze band above the home page's vision section, and the glaze band that opens the Give page. The interface itself stays in clay, iron and cobalt. Density is low and the pace is unhurried. The ministry's own words (mission, values, vision, the closing invitation) are centered and given room; practical content (what happens when you ask, who we help, roles, costs, stories) sits in asymmetric two-column spreads. Eleven inner pages extend the home page through four layout families (the reference page, the audience page, the photograph appeal and the form page) without adding a material. The world refuses the glossy cosmetic-surgery ad, the clip-art church bulletin, the pity appeal and the cold hospital portal.

The people this site serves may arrive hesitant, on a phone, with a disability, or reading Spanish first, so the world is built to fail gracefully. Every brushed mark is painted by default and animates only as an enhancement. Motion stops for visitors who ask for less of it. Raster-drawn controls fall back to system borders in forced-colors mode. Fonts are self-hosted, so loading the page tells no third party about the visit. The site is honest about what is not settled: every fact the ministry has not confirmed carries a dashed Placeholder tag, and every demo form says plainly that nothing is sent.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A buff stoneware ground under a tiled orange-peel texture; never a flat white page.
- Iron-brown serif (Tiro Devanagari Marathi, 400 roman and italic only) for headings, buttons, links and quotations; Mulish Variable for body copy, form text and spaced capitals.
- Cobalt slip is the only accent: actions, their interaction states, and brushed ornament, including the brushed underline on links.
- Brushed marks are raster plates with recorded provenance, never CSS imitations. A plate can be used as a mask so its color follows the ground.
- One motion: a cobalt line drawing itself on, like slip trailed from a bulb.
- Flat surfaces. Depth comes from photography and the dark iron band, used at most once per page.
- Unconfirmed facts wear a dashed Placeholder tag; samples wear a dashed Sample tag.
- WCAG 2.2 AA (axe reports no violations on any page at 1440 and 390 wide, form error states included), reduced motion respected, forced-colors fallbacks, no overlay widgets.

## Colors

The palette is fired clay: a warm buff ground, iron-brown inks at three strengths, one saturated cobalt, a dark iron band, and, for forms only, a clay-white well and an iron-oxide red.

### Primary
- **Cobalt Slip** (#0b3d9c): every action and every brushed ornament. It fills the Ask for help and Give swatches, the send buttons and the chosen gift amount; colors inline action links and paints their brushed underline; colors the other-language link and footer links on hover, the current and hovered jump-list entries, and the current page in the narrow menu; draws the focus ring, the text caret, text selection and the accent of native radios and checkboxes; and paints the brushed rules (header rule, mission dash, value strokes, step dividers, nav stroke, jump dab, gift frequency stroke). 6.4:1 on the ground, 4.4:1 on the texture's darkest buff, 8.5:1 on Clay White.
- **Slip White** (#fbf8f2): the label on cobalt swatches (including the chosen gift amount), the skip link and text selection (9.2:1 on cobalt).

### Neutral
- **Salt-Glazed Buff** (#d8d1c4): the page ground and the browser `theme-color`. It always sits under the ground texture tile (308 × 204px, WebP with PNG fallback); the flat color is only the tile's fallback.
- **Buff Shade** (#c9c0b1) and **Buff Deep** (#b9ad9b): the scrollbar track and thumb. Buff Deep is also the darkest fleck in the texture, so every text color is checked against it as well as the ground.
- **Iron** (#3a1f0e): headings, the wordmark, navigation, jump-list and footer links, the ink-frame button label, field labels, the placeholder tag's text, italic asides and quotations. 10:1 on the ground.
- **Iron Soft** (#4f3a2a): spaced-capital labels, the language switch, attributions, notes, field hints, the dashed border of tags and notices, and the footer base row. 7:1 on the ground, 4.8:1 on Buff Deep.
- **Clay Text** (#3c3736): running body copy, ledes and the text typed into fields. 7.7:1 on the ground, 10.2:1 on Clay White.
- **Iron Hairline** (rgb(58 31 14 / 0.14)): 1px structural rules between home-page sections and between narrow-menu links. The same iron rises to 18% for the footer base row, 20% between chosen photos, 22% between conditions, 25% under each "what we ask" item, 30% for form sections, jump-list entries and the form aside, 35% for footer link underlines and 40% between roles.
- **Section Hairline** (rgb(58 31 14 / 0.5)): the 1px rule that opens each section on the inner pages: reference sections, audience splits, stories, the colleague's letter and the About card.

### Iron band
The home page's vision section and the Give page's Sponsor a surgery section are set on the iron of the clay body: **Kiln Iron** (#2a170c) with the ground texture multiplied over it.
- **Band Ink** (#efe6d6): the vision statement, path names, the Sponsor title and cost line, the on-dark ink frame and its label, and a placeholder tag's text on the band (13.8:1).
- **Band Ink Soft** (#ddd1bd): path descriptions, Sponsor body text, and a placeholder tag's dashed border on the band (11.4:1).
- **Band Label** (#d4c6ae): the spaced-capital label on the band (10.2:1).
- **Link on Iron** (#f6efe3) with a **Cobalt Wash** stroke (#8fa8dc): inline links on the band take light ink (15:1) and a lighter cobalt brushed stroke (7.2:1), because cobalt on Kiln Iron falls to 1.8:1.
- **Band Hairline** (rgb(239 230 214 / 0.28)): the rule above each get-involved path.

### Forms
- **Clay White** (#f4efe6): the well of every text field, select and text area.
- **Umber Edge** (#6e5a4b): the 1px edge of a field (5.7:1 against the well, 4.3:1 against the ground).
- **Iron Oxide** (#74200e): an answer that needs a change. It draws the 2px border of an invalid field, the outline of an invalid choice, the error message, the error summary's border, title and links, and the photo notice. 7.1:1 on the ground, 9.4:1 on Clay White, 4.9:1 on the texture's darkest buff.

### Named Rules
**The One Slip Rule.** Cobalt is reserved for things a visitor can act on, their interaction states, and the brushed ornament. It never colors a heading, body copy, an icon or a background panel.

**The Glaze Stays in the Kiln Rule.** Amber and olive ash glaze appear only inside photographs of fired stoneware: the hero jar, the home glaze band and the Give glaze band. They are never sampled into CSS for text, fills, borders or hover states.

**The Darker Label Rule.** Spaced-capital labels use Iron Soft (#4f3a2a), deliberately darker than the comp's measured gray (#927e6f, 2.5:1 on the ground), so the smallest text on the page clears WCAG AA even over the texture's darkest flecks.

**The Oxide Rule.** Iron Oxide marks only an answer that needs a change. It is never decoration, emphasis, or a warning about anything else.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Tiro Devanagari Marathi (with Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Mulish Variable (with Mulish, Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)

Both faces are self-hosted through @fontsource and loaded in `angular.json`: Tiro Devanagari Marathi at 400 roman and 400 italic, Latin subset only; Mulish Variable on its full weight axis (200 to 1000). There is no Google Fonts or other font CDN request.

**Character:** A warm, slightly rustic book serif with generous proportions carries the voice: headings, buttons, links, quotations and the hero's place line. A soft humanist sans carries explanation, form labels and the spaced capitals. Emphasis in the serif is italic, never bold.

- **Section Label** (Mulish 650, clamp(0.875rem, 0.85rem + 0.2vw, 1.0625rem), 0.33em, uppercase): the heading of a section whose body is the ministry's own words (Our Mission, Our Core Values, Our Vision) on the home page, the audience pages and the About card. The card's connective "Therefore" and the letter's signature take the same 0.33em tracking at Label size. A centered label carries a negative end margin equal to its tracking so it centers optically.
- **Small** (Mulish 400 or 650, 0.875rem): notes, the placeholder note, the footer base row; the strong weight sets the sample tag. Demo notices and the required-fields line are a touch larger at 0.9375rem.
- **Tag** (Mulish 650, 0.8125rem, 1.4, 0.02em): the Placeholder tag.

Headings use `text-wrap: balance` and paragraphs `text-wrap: pretty`, so staff-edited copy and longer Spanish strings re-break cleanly.

### Named Rules
**The No-Kicker Rule.** Spaced capitals never sit above a heading as a kicker or eyebrow. They are the heading itself (a section label over a quotation or statement), a footer group heading, a detail term, or an attribution. The hero has no eyebrow: who and where is said in a sentence-case italic place line under the actions.

**The One-Weight Serif Rule.** Tiro Devanagari Marathi ships one weight. Headings are set at 400 and take their presence from size; never apply bold to the serif.

**The Self-Hosted Type Rule.** Fonts load from the app bundle through @fontsource, never from a font CDN, so a visit is not reported to a third party.

## Layout

Sections stack on the stoneware ground. They are separated by space, by a 1px iron hairline, or by the iron band; never by cards or panels. A single fluid gutter (`{spacing.gutter}`) holds every section's inline padding. Standard sections breathe at `{spacing.section}` top and bottom; open, centered sections at `{spacing.section-open}`. Home-page sections run full-bleed with full-width hairlines; inner-page sections are inset by the gutter, so their Section Hairline stops at the text edge.

Two composition modes carry the whole site. **Centered** sets the ministry's own words: mission, values, vision, the closing invitation, the footer scripture, the opening statement of an audience page, the About card and the Give appeal. **Asymmetric split** sets practical content: on the home page, 5fr / 7fr for what happens when you ask and who we help, with the left intro (max 30rem) sticky at `top: 2rem` from 62em, and 8fr / 4fr for stories, aligned to the bottom. Every sticky column on the site sticks at `{spacing.sticky-top}`.

The home hero splits at 62em into a text column (`minmax(0, 1fr)`) and a jar column (50%). The jar bleeds to the right edge, its photograph masked to fade in from the left (0 to 14%) and at top and bottom (7% and 96%), and a negative bottom margin (-3.27vw) lets the mission band rise into its shadow. Below 62em the page goes to one column: headline, lede and both actions first, then the jar cutout centered at min(86%, 34rem).

**The Action-in-Reach Rule.** On a reference page the primary action rides in the sticky aside from 62em and repeats inline below it, so the way to ask is never far from the answer that prompted it.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Surfaces never lift. Depth comes from three places: the photographs' own light and contact shadows (the jar's soft shadow is part of the picture, and its masked edges dissolve into the ground; the Give glaze band fades into the ground from 72%); tone, in the dark iron band that holds the home vision and Give's Sponsor a surgery, with the home glaze photograph fading into it through a mask from 62%; and the texture tile, which gives the whole page a fired surface.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Menu drape** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 30px -18px rgb(58 31 14 / 0.45)`): the narrow-screen menu panel only, so it reads as lying over the page under the header.

### Named Rules
**The Fired Surface Rule.** Content gets no cards, panels or drop shadows. Separation comes from space, a 1px iron hairline, or the iron band. The only filled boxes are form wells; notices are outlines (dashed Iron Soft for demo and draft notices, 2px Iron Oxide for the error summary).

## Shapes

Edges are made by hand, not by `border-radius`. Primary actions are cobalt swatches whose dry-brush edges come from a raster (ask-brush, give-brush) painted just outside the label box. The secondary action is a hand-inked iron frame, nine-sliced from the ink-frame raster (13px border, slice 46) so its corners keep their scale at any width; on the iron band it is drawn from ink-frame-light, the same frame recolored to Band Ink. The ornamental lines are brushed rasters too: the full-width rule (under the header, mirrored over the footer, and 6px tall at 85% opacity between steps and over a brushed split), the short dash under a section label (also the marker above each value at one column, and under the letter), and the same dash turned upright as the value stroke.

The newer marks all come from the mission dash: link-stroke is the dash trimmed to its ink (1281 × 129), used as a CSS mask under links and nav links and as the bullet in Privacy and Accessibility lists; jump-mark is the dash resampled to 840 × 181 for the jump-list dab; toggle-stroke is link-stroke resampled onto a 1281 × 116 canvas, under the chosen gift frequency. The chosen gift amount is a give-brush swatch (amount-brush), and unchosen amounts sit in the ink frame. Structural lines are plain 1px hairlines.

The only CSS radii are the 2px focus ring and the 3px corner of the dashed tags and notices (sample tag, placeholder tag, demo and draft notices). Form wells and the error summary are square. The jar's silhouette is the page's one large organic shape.

**The Two Lines Rule.** Brushed cobalt lines are raster plates and carry rhythm and state: the header rule, the mission dash, the value strokes, the step dividers, the link stroke, the jump dab. Separation between sections and list items is a 1px iron hairline. A plate may be used as a mask to recolor it, but its shape always comes from the brush. Never imitate the brush with CSS gradients or SVG filters.

### Brush Reveal (signature motion)
- **Keyframe:** `brush-draw` animates `clip-path` from `inset(0 100% 0 0)` to `inset(0)`, so brushed lines draw on left to right; the value strokes draw top to bottom.
- **Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for every animation and transition.
- **Timings:** header rule 1100ms after 150ms at first paint; mission dash 800ms after 750ms (audience dash after 600ms); current nav stroke 750ms at load; jump dab 650ms after 300ms; footer rule 1200ms; step rules 1000ms, 140ms apart; value strokes 900ms, 110ms apart after 120ms. Strokes drawn by interaction take 420ms (gift frequency) and 520ms (nav hover). Other interaction transitions run 160ms to 220ms.
- **Behavior:** the `bfaBrushReveal` directive (on the values, the step lists and the footer) arms a section only when its top is below 92% of the viewport at first render, then draws it once 20% of it is visible. Strokes are painted by default; nothing is armed when JavaScript fails, IntersectionObserver is missing, or reduced motion is requested, and a global reduced-motion rule collapses every animation and transition to 0.01ms.
- Nothing else moves: no parallax, no scroll-driven effects, no fades on text.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** lay every page on the stoneware ground: Salt-Glazed Buff (#d8d1c4) under the 308 × 204px texture tile.
- **Do** keep Cobalt Slip (#0b3d9c) for actions, their interaction states (focus ring, selection, caret, hover, current) and brushed ornament.
- **Do** pair the brushed cobalt swatch for Ask for help with the ink frame for Refer someone, in that order.
- **Do** underline every link outside the footer with the brushed link stroke, used as a mask so it takes cobalt on the ground and Cobalt Wash (#8fa8dc) on the iron band; keep footer links on plain iron underlines.
- **Do** draw brushed marks from raster plates, shipped as WebP with a PNG fallback through `image-set()`, and record provenance for every new raster.
- **Do** set spaced-capital labels in Iron Soft (#4f3a2a) or darker, and check every text color against the texture's darkest buff (#b9ad9b) as well as the ground.
- **Do** use Link on Iron (#f6efe3) with the Cobalt Wash stroke (#8fa8dc) for links on the iron band, and the Band Ink frame for a secondary action there.
- **Do** mark every fact the ministry has not confirmed with the Placeholder tag, and end a page that carries tags with one note explaining them.
- **Do** say on every demo form that nothing is sent: the dashed notice above the request, referral, sign-up and message forms, a tagged line under the Give button. Keep it until the HIPAA-compliant service and Stripe are connected.
- **Do** move focus to the error summary after every failed send, link each entry to its control, and tie hints and errors to controls with `aria-describedby`.
- **Do** keep radios and checkboxes native, tinted with a cobalt `accent-color`.
- **Do** paint every stroke by default and animate it only as an enhancement; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** give every raster-drawn control a forced-colors fallback: a 2px `ButtonText` border with the raster hidden, and a system underline where a masked stroke disappears.
- **Do** label every sample story and letter visibly until real, consented material replaces it.
- **Do** put the headline, lede and actions before the photograph on narrow screens.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put a spaced-capital kicker or eyebrow above a heading; say who and where in a sentence-case line instead.
- **Don't** use amber, olive or any glaze color outside photography.
- **Don't** set cobalt text or links on the iron band (1.8:1).
- **Don't** use Iron Oxide (#74200e) for anything but an answer that needs a change.
- **Don't** lighten labels to the comp's gray (#927e6f); it fails AA on the ground.
- **Don't** bold the serif or substitute a system display face.
- **Don't** load fonts from Google Fonts or any other CDN; self-host them through @fontsource.
- **Don't** give content cards, panels, rounded action shapes or drop shadows; the menu drape is the only cast shadow, and form wells are the only filled boxes.
- **Don't** imitate the brush with CSS gradients or SVG filters.
- **Don't** add icon rows or icon glyphs; the inline two-stroke menu icon is the only icon.
- **Don't** state an unconfirmed fact without its Placeholder tag, or present a sample as real.
- **Don't** install an accessibility overlay widget; accessibility is built into the markup and styles.
- **Don't** reach for sunsets, clip-art crosses, before-and-after spectacle or pity imagery.
